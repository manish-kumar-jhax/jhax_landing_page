# JHAX.ai — 3-Minute VC Demo Script

Verbal script for the live product demo. Words to say verbatim (italics = stage direction / not spoken). Time budget: **3 minutes**. If they interrupt with a question, answer, then jump to the next screen.

---

## Open — 0:00 to 0:20 (20 sec)

*Land on the hero. Do not scroll. The demo card is animating in the top-right — typewriter is cycling.*

> "Independent restaurants — 1 to 15 locations — lose about **$2,400 a week** without ever seeing it happen. Overstaffing on Tuesday lunch. A VIP who quietly stops coming back. A campaign nobody measured.
>
> They already have a POS. They already have QuickBooks. They already have a WhatsApp business account. What they don't have is anyone who watches all of them, at the same time, at 2 a.m. — and does something about it.
>
> That's what we built. **JHAX is an AI COO.**"

*Point at the demo card as the question changes on the countdown.*

> "This is live. Right now it's asking questions a real operator asks — customers, menu, labor, marketing — and answering them with the customer's own Square data. Sixty seconds per question."

## Three questions to expect — the exact answers

**Q: "Isn't this just Square with a nicer dashboard?"**
> "Square gives you a dashboard and leaves you the work. JHAX gives you a decision and takes the work. Different product. The loop slide shows exactly where they stop and where we go."

**Q: "How do you defend against Square or Toast building this themselves?"**
> "Two things. One — they've had ten years. They haven't. Their business model is transaction fees, not operator outcomes. Two — the moat isn't the AI, it's the **DEPLOY and MEASURE** steps. That means we sit on top of every POS, not one. Toast can't afford to make you a better Square customer; we can."

**Q: "What's the wedge? Why will restaurants switch?"**
> "They don't switch. That's the point. We plug into Square. Their staff logs in Monday, sees three overstaffed shifts saving $316, taps the button, and by Tuesday they've paid for the year. We're not replacing anything — we're finishing the job everyone else abandoned."

## Closing line — verbatim

> "The last thing I'll say is what I show them on their first call. I ask them one question — 'Can you tell me, right now, without opening any app, why your Tuesday sales were down last week?' Every one of them says no. That's the product. That's the round. That's why we're moving fast."

*Pause. Do not fill the silence. Wait for the next question.*

## Contingency — if the demo is fed only 60 seconds

Cut to: Hero (5s) → Loop section (25s) → Final CTA + $82.7B stat (30s). Skip Sections 01, 02, 03. The moat slide alone will do 80% of the work.

Numbers to have on the tip of the tongue:
- ACV: **$3,588/yr** ($299 × 12)
- Gross margin: **~85%** (LLM + Square API + hosting)
- Payback: typically 1 Tuesday of recovered labor waste
- LTV: >$15k assuming 3-yr retention; renewal driver is measured $ recovered
- CAC target: <$400 via operator communities + WhatsApp broker referral
